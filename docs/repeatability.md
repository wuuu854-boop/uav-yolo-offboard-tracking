# Repeatability of the recent doorway tasks

A single SITL pass is not evidence of robustness. The retained suite repeats the
doorway missions, restarting the simulator between runs, and keeps every failure
that led to a configuration change.

The rendered table is [`results/repeat_report.md`](../results/repeat_report.md).

## Results

| Scenario | Runs | PASS | Target error min/mean/max (m) | Clearance min/max (m) | Contacts | Landings |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---:|---:|
| gate | 5 | **5/5** | 0.133 / 0.489 / 0.783 | 0.810 / 1.176 | 0 | 5/5 |
| gate-double | 5 | **5/5** | 0.167 / 0.260 / 0.350 | 0.544 / 0.708 | 0 | 5/5 |
| gate-target1, original 0.5 m / 0.2 m | 3 | **0/3** | 0.364 / 0.393 / 0.408 | 0.018 / 0.384 | **4** | 3/3 |
| gate-target1, fixed 0.8 m / 0.1 m | 10 | **9/10** | 0.043 / 0.287 / 0.612 | 0.667 / 0.776 | **1** | 10/10 |
| gate-target1, retained 1.0 m / 0.05 m | 10 | **10/10** | 0.133 / 0.307 / 0.524 | 0.786 / 0.943 | 0 | 10/10 |

The progression is the point:

- **0.5/0.2** was not just single-run flaky; it failed **0/3** in repeats.
- **0.8/0.1** improved it to **9/10** but still had one contact at 0.704 m
  reported clearance. A 5/5 sample had hidden that failure.
- **1.0/0.05** is **10/10**, with 0.786 m minimum clearance. This is now the
  retained gate-target1 mission.

The earlier target1 single pass (0.42 m clearance) and the 0.8/0.1 9/10 evidence
are both preserved in `results/repeat/`; none of the failures are hidden.

## What is verified, and what is not

| Claim | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| gate 5/5 repeats | ✅ verified | 0 contacts, 0.810 m minimum |
| gate-double 5/5 repeats | ✅ verified | 0 contacts, 0.544 m minimum |
| gate-target1 retained 1.0/0.05, 10/10 | ✅ verified | 0 contacts, 0.786 m minimum |
| gate-target1 0.8/0.1 | ⚠️ **9/10, one contact** | not robust enough |
| gate-target1 original 0.5/0.2 | ❌ **0/3** | 4 contacts |
| Larger sample / confidence interval | ❌ **not done** | n = 10 target1, n = 5 others |
| Hardware / real sensor | ❌ **not done** | SITL, ideal depth |

## Evidence

- `results/repeat_report.md` and `results/repeat_report.json` — the table above.
- `results/repeat/<scenario>_<n>_summary.json` — 33 per-run reports (5 + 5 + 3 +
  10 + 10).
- `results/repeat/<scenario>_<n>_landing.csv` — per-run landing row.
- `results/repeat/*.log` — per-run offboard/depth logs (kept locally; not all
  copied into git).

## Reproducing it

```bash
make repeat5             # gate, gate-target1 (retained), gate-double 5x
make repeat-target1-10   # ten runs of the retained gate-target1 margin mission
```

The retained gate-target1 mission is `config/mission_gate_target1.yaml`
(1.0 m inflation, 0.05 m waypoint tolerance). The 0.8/0.1 and 0.5/0.2 samples are
historical evidence and are not regenerated by these targets. n is still small
and the whole suite is SITL-only.
